5. EXIT: Target trades reached, setup degraded, max trades hit, or manual stop.
6. PAUSE: Max 5 bunches per hour. After enough bunches, cool down until next hour.

BUNCH STAKE SCALING:
- Starting stake from setup score (≥70: $1.50, else $1.00)
- Compound multiplier from GrowthEngine
- Replicator boost on winning patterns
- PLManager trailing stop

MARKET STATE CLASSIFICATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MarketStateBrain classifies each market every cycle:
- STRONG_TREND: High momentum, clear direction → TRADE with trend
- WEAK_TREND: Mild momentum → REDUCE_SIZE, trade cautiously
- RANGING: No clear direction, oscillating → WAIT for breakout
- COMPRESSION: Low volatility, tight range → REDUCE_SIZE or WAIT
- CALM: Very low activity → WAIT
- ANOMALOUS: Gap/spike/reversal/chop detected → SKIP
- UNTRADABLE: Multiple anomalies → FULLY SKIP

Each state outputs: action (TRADE/REDUCE_SIZE/WAIT/SKIP) + confidence %.
